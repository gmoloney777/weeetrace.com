Privacy notice

Last updated 27/10/2026

This notice explains what information the WEEETrace website (weeetrace.com) collects and what happens to it. It covers this website only, not the WEEETrace app or platform.

Who we are

WEEETrace is responsible for the information described here. You can contact us about anything in this notice at hello@weeetrace.com.

What this website collects

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or any tracking, and doesn't load anything from other websites. Its fonts and images are served from weeetrace.com itself.

Like any website, the company that hosts it may keep short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure. We don't use those logs to identify you.

When you email us

The demo form on our home page doesn't send or store anything. It opens your own email app with a message to hello@weeetrace.com, which you can check and edit before you send it.

If you email us, we receive what you choose to include, usually:

  • your name and email address;
  • your organisation and what it handles;
  • anything you write in your message.

We use this only to reply to you and to arrange a demo if you've asked for one. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries about WEEETrace. We don't sell your information or add you to a mailing list.

We keep your emails for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry, and for no more than 2 years after we were last in touch.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it or stop using it. Email hello@weeetrace.com and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
