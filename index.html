01 / How it works

From a pallet of paperwork to proof, one meter at a time.

Follow one pallet of removed meters through WEEETrace.

  1. 01 · Goods-in In build

    A pallet arrives. The paperwork says 48.

    Removed meters arrive by the pallet or stillage, with a supplier manifest that lists part numbers and quantities, not serial numbers, and it's often wrong. Every meter on it still has to be accounted for.

    The operator starts goods-in on the phone: the consignment reference, then the pallet's label exactly as found.

  2. 02 · Capture

    The camera reads the plate. The operator confirms.

    Point the phone at the meter. WEEETrace reads the meter serial number from the rating plate and the supplier's asset label in the same view, and checks each value against known meter formats. Nothing is saved until the operator confirms it.

    • Green tick: the value matches its rules.
    • Orange exclamation: missing, or needs a look.
    • Fuel and generation come from the model catalogue.
  3. 03 · Record

    Every meter becomes its own record, even with no signal.

    Each confirmed meter is saved with its consignment, pallet, label photo and time, and starts an append-only custody timeline: corrections are added, never written over.

    Warehouses and yards are often dead spots, so capture carries on offline and syncs when the phone reconnects. Then any meter can be found on the web by any of its identifiers.

  4. 04 · Checks

    Duplicates and unknown models are flagged on the spot.

    If a serial has been seen before, on this phone or any other in your organisation, the operator is told straight away, even offline, and decides whether it's the same meter.

    A model that isn't in the catalogue is flagged too, so it gets added rather than guessed.

  5. 05 · Sort and value Coming

    Each meter goes to its owner's pallet. You see what the load is worth.

    Upload each meter asset provider's asset list, and every scan shows which outbound pallet the meter goes on, or flags it as foreign or of unknown owner. Smart meters with lithium batteries get a hazard flag.

    The model catalogue turns the load into an estimated value, net of the cost of breaking it down, and the counted pallet is reconciled against the supplier's manifest.

  6. 06 · Prove and report Coming

    Proof for your client. Receipts to Defra on time.

    Each job ends with a versioned certificate that carries a QR code anyone can verify, and your clients see their own meters in a free portal.

    Receipts are reported to Defra's Digital Waste Tracking service, with the two-day deadline tracked for you.

02 / Why per meter

Meters are tracked by serial number. Most waste software counts loads.

  • 01

    Load-level tools stop at the weighbridge.

    Most waste-tracking software works per consignment: a ticket, a weight, a code. Meter work happens per serial number, and so do the questions owners and clients ask.

  • 02

    Nothing reads the serial for you.

    Once a meter is off the wall, its serial has to be read from the faceplate, which is often worn or dirty. WEEETrace's camera is built for exactly those labels, and the operator stays in charge of every value.

  • 03

    Ownership is the expensive part.

    Getting each removed meter back to the right owner is costly and often confused. A record per meter turns ownership into a check at the bench, not a guess at the end.
